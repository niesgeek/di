The service服务内容

DINO Mail gives you an email address on di.fs.kg, ip.fs.kg, or ok.fs.kg, a web mailbox to read and write in, and — if you want them — forwarding and Telegram notifications. DINO Mail 为你提供 di.fs.kg、ip.fs.kg 或 ok.fs.kg 上的一个邮箱地址、一个收发信的网页邮箱，以及（如果你需要）转发和 Telegram 通知。

It is free, run by one person in their own time, and offered as it is. Registration needs an invite code. An invite is a courtesy, not a purchase: it carries no entitlement to uptime, capacity, or permanence. 它免费、由一个人利用自己的时间维护、按现状提供。注册需要邀请码。邀请是一份人情，不是一笔交易：它不附带任何关于在线率、容量或永久存在的承诺。

Your account你的账户

  • One account per person. Not one per alias, and not a shared login.一人一个账户。不是每个别名一个，也不是几个人共用一个。
  • You must be old enough to agree to this on your own — 13, or the minimum age in your country.你需要达到能自行同意本条款的年龄——13 岁，或你所在地区法定的最低年龄。
  • Keep your password to yourself. What happens under your account is your responsibility.密码自己收好。账户下发生的事由你负责。
  • Tell us promptly if you think someone else has it.如果怀疑密码泄露，请尽快告诉我们。

Acceptable use使用规范

Please do not use this mailbox to: 请不要用这个邮箱做以下事情：

  • Send unsolicited bulk mail, chain letters, or anything that reads like spam.发送未经请求的批量邮件、连锁信，或任何读起来像垃圾邮件的东西。
  • Phish, spoof, or impersonate anyone — including in the From: header.钓鱼、伪造或冒充他人——包括伪造 From: 头。
  • Distribute malware, or link to it.传播恶意软件，或给出它的链接。
  • Do anything illegal under the laws that apply to you.做任何违反你所在地法律的事。
  • Harass, threaten, or stalk a person.骚扰、威胁或跟踪他人。
  • Register accounts in bulk, or resell addresses.批量注册账户，或倒卖邮箱地址。
  • Probe, scan, overload the service, or work around its rate limits.探测、扫描、压垮服务，或绕过限流。
  • Use the domain as a return path to launder another service's sending reputation.把这个域名当作回信地址，替别的服务洗白发信信誉。

Automated sending, scraping address lists, and high-volume mail are all out of bounds. An account doing it gets suspended first and explained to later — that is the only order that works at this size. 自动群发、抓取地址列表、大流量发信，全都越界。这类账户会先被停用，事后再解释——在这个体量下，只有这个顺序行得通。

Your mail is yours邮件属于你

What you write and what you receive belongs to you. You keep every right to it. 你写下的和你收到的，都属于你。全部权利归你。

You give us only the permission needed to run the service: to store your mail, transmit it, show it back to you, and — if you switch them on — forward it or send a summary to Telegram. That permission ends when the message is deleted or the account is closed. 你只授予我们运行服务所必需的许可：保存你的邮件、把它发出去、在界面上呈现给你，以及（在你打开开关时）转发它或向 Telegram 发送摘要。这封邮件被删除或账户被注销时，该许可即终止。

We claim no ownership, we license your mail to nobody else, and we do not read it — except when you ask us to fix something, or when we must to stop clear abuse. 我们不主张所有权，不向任何第三方转授你的邮件，也不去读它——除非你请我们修某个问题，或为了制止明显的滥用而不得不看。

Sending limits发送限制

Outbound mail goes through Resend and is subject to their limits and reputation rules. On top of that, each account is rate-limited here. Hitting a limit slows or pauses sending — it does not mean your mailbox is broken, and it lifts on its own. 发信经由 Resend，受其限额与信誉规则约束。除此之外，本站对每个账户另有速率限制。触到限额会让发信变慢或暂停——这不代表邮箱坏了，限制会自行解除。

Suspension and closing停用与注销

We may suspend or close an account that breaks these terms, that damages the service or its sending reputation, or that we are legally required to act on. Where it is practical, you will be told why and given a chance to take your mail with you first. 对于违反本条款、损害服务或其发信信誉、或法律要求我们处理的账户，我们可能予以停用或注销。在可行的情况下，会告知原因，并先给你把邮件带走的机会。

You can stop using DINO Mail at any time. Ask, and the account and its mail will be deleted. 你随时可以不再使用 DINO Mail。说一声，账户和里面的邮件就会被删除。
